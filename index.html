Profile

Hyacinth is a results-driven social media marketing manager with over 6 years of experience creating impactful social media strategies that enhance brand visibility, drive audience engagement, and increase sales — across food, fashion, gadgets, furniture, and beauty services.

She's a former entrepreneur — she started her first business at 13 and was running her own shop by 16 in General Santos City, Philippines. That hands-on business ownership is what shapes how she works now: she understands the struggles of business owners firsthand and is passionate about helping brands thrive through practical, innovative social media solutions rather than generic playbooks.

Today she works with brands across fashion e-commerce, children's publishing, SaaS, medical aesthetics, and beauty & wellness — producing content, running paid and organic campaigns, managing influencer/UGC partnerships, and in several cases building a brand's entire digital presence and marketing strategy from the ground up as their brand & growth marketing manager.
